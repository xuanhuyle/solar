Company: Coastline Grocers
Profile: Coastline Grocers runs 140 grocery stores along the coast and in two inland cities, with online ordering and click-and-collect from most stores.
Forecast mandate: each day, forecast the next day's 24 hourly values of checkout transactions across all stores (thousands per hour).
Study period 3: days 1-126 are available for research in three rounds (days 1-84, 1-112, 1-126).
Store format mix in this study period: Format mix A: mostly large-format stores, weekly-shop focus

Data dictionary (each source's values for a day are known before that day starts):
- Promotions and pricing:
  - X02: Planned promotion intensity. Share of the range on promotion for the coming day, from the marketing plan.
  - X03: Recorded promotion intensity. Share of the range on promotion for the coming day, as loaded into the store pricing system.
  - X04: Competitor discount index. Index of announced competitor discounts for the coming day, from a price-monitoring service.
- Local movement:
  - X06: Footfall forecast. Day-ahead hourly footfall forecast near the stores, from a mobile-data provider.
  - X08: Public-transport ridership forecast. Day-ahead hourly ridership forecast for the bus and tram lines serving the stores.
  - X05: Local events index. Hourly index of scheduled local events near the stores.
- Supply chain:
  - X01: Delivery-slot fill rate. Share of online delivery slots booked for the coming day.
  - X07: Stock-availability index. Planned on-shelf availability for the coming day.

Operating and event log:
- Day -25: The city councils changed bus and tram timetables and pedestrianised several shopping streets.
- Day -12: Two competitors announced a long-running discount campaign in our main regions.

Research question: Which of the eight information sources currently carry predictive information for checkout transactions across all stores (thousands per hour), and what does the evidence support?
